The world's most famous white vineyard, split almost exactly between Puligny and Chassagne. The Marquis de Laguiche's quarter has been in the family since 1363 and is made by Drouhin.

One of the 4 grands crus of Puligny-Montrachet on INAO's map, which together cover 30.9 ha.

Soil: Hard limestone with a thin layer of brown soil; perfect drainage
