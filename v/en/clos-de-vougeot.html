The Cistercians' 50-ha walled vineyard, now ~80 owners. Top of the slope (next to Musigny & Grands-Échezeaux) is the prize; the bottom by the road is flatter and heavier — who owns where matters more than the label.

Soil: Top: pebbly limestone; middle: clay-limestone; bottom by the road: deep alluvial clay
