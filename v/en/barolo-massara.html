Massara is one of the 170 menzioni geografiche aggiuntive (MGA) of Barolo, named areas set down in the production rules: on the label it follows the denomination, as “Barolo Massara”. A vineyard's own name may follow it as “vigna …”, at a lower yield (7.2 t/ha). The rules also allow 11 village mentions.
