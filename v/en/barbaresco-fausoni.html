Fausoni is one of the 66 menzioni geografiche aggiuntive (MGA) of Barbaresco, named areas set down in the production rules: on the label it follows the denomination, as “Barbaresco Fausoni”. A vineyard's own name may follow it as “vigna …”, at a lower yield (7.2 t/ha). The rules also allow 0 village mentions.
