Planted by the monks of Bèze in 630 AD — the oldest recorded clos on the Côte. Silkier and more aromatic than Chambertin; may also be sold as plain Chambertin.

One of the 10 grands crus of Gevrey-Chambertin on INAO's map, which together cover 88.1 ha.

Soil: Brown clay-limestone over Bajocian marl, pebbly; good drainage
