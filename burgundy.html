About the data. The map is schematic: each commune is drawn on its own grid with the slope running west (top of the hill) to east (the D974), so neighbours, slope position and rough size are right but the shapes are not a cadastral survey. Areas are the official climat sizes (≈). Holdings are compiled from published ownership lists, domaine sources and trade press (each domaine panel lists its sources) and rounded. "Leased" marks vines farmed under a fermage or métayage; "buys grapes" marks négociant sourcing, which is never counted as ownership — parcels are bought, swapped and leased all the time, so treat them as indicative; owners shown without a figure are known holders whose exact area isn't listed.
