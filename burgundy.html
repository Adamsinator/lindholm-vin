About the data. Areas are the official climat sizes (≈). Holdings come from published ownership lists, domaine sources and trade press (each domaine lists its own) and are rounded; parcels are bought, swapped and leased all the time, so treat them as indicative. "Leased" means farmed under a fermage or métayage; "buys grapes" is négociant sourcing and never counts as ownership; a holder without a figure is a known owner whose area isn't published. Corton's red climats count as one appellation, and Charlemagne isn't drawn on its own. Logged-in users can correct a holding from its vineyard.
