About the data. Vineyard boundaries are INAO's parcel delimitation; roads, buildings, woods, contours and place names are IGN's BD TOPO (both Licence Ouverte). Areas are published climat sizes, checked against the Climats UNESCO dossier; the INAO area is shown beside them. Holdings come from published ownership lists, domaine sources and trade press, rounded — parcels change hands all the time, so treat them as indicative. "Leased" means farmed en fermage or métayage; "buys grapes" is négociant sourcing and never counts as ownership. Logged-in users can correct a holding from its vineyard.
